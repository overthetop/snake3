# Snake

The vocabulary of a classic Snake game: steering a growing snake around a board to eat food without colliding with a wall or itself.

## Language

**Snake**:
The player-controlled creature whose length increases when it eats food.
_Avoid_: Player (when referring to the creature)

**Board**:
The grid of cells in which the snake moves, bounded by lethal walls.
_Avoid_: Map, level, arena

**Cell**:
A single position on the board's grid.
_Avoid_: Tile, square

**Food**:
The collectible the snake eats to grow and increase its score.
_Avoid_: Power-up, bonus

**Score**:
The points earned by eating food during play.
_Avoid_: Experience, currency

**Collision**:
Contact with a wall or the snake's own body that ends play.
_Avoid_: Damage, hit points

**Run**:
A single attempt to earn a score, beginning with a new snake and ending in a loss or completion.
_Avoid_: Match, round, session

**Win**:
Completion of a run by filling every cell of the board with the snake.
_Avoid_: Level clear, next level

**Best score**:
The highest score earned across runs on this device, retained between launches.
_Avoid_: High-score table, leaderboard, record

**Pause**:
A temporary suspension of a run during which the snake does not advance.
_Avoid_: Stop, game over

**Speed**:
The rate at which the snake advances across cells, increasing as it eats food.
_Avoid_: Frame rate, difficulty level
